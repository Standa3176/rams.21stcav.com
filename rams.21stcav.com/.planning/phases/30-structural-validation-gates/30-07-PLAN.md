Build GATE-13 (hot-works contradiction) whole, with both halves and full test coverage, behind its
own `RAMS_HOT_WORKS_GATE` flag which stays false. Phase 31 flips it.

Purpose: the concrete real-world defect is the 21CQ30960 three-way contradiction — RA18 says no hot
works, §6.8 requires a hot-works permit for soldering, and COSHH carries Tin/Lead solder and rosin
flux. Three sections disagreeing inside one safety document.

The gate cannot be armed today and D-02 is emphatic about why. The COSHH half is blocked by
`Tier1RamsDefaultsService::injectDefaultsIntoRamsData()` setting `$data['coshh_baseline']`
unconditionally at `:81` from a baseline carrying Tin/Lead Solder (`config/rams_tier1.php:162`) and
Rosin Flux (`:173`), so every generated RAMS lists solder and flux. Research Finding 5 found the
permit half is blocked one layer deeper: `addPermitAndIsolation()` emits its hot-works-permit line
unconditionally, on every document, inside `upgrade()` itself at `:939`. Both halves would fire
corpus-wide. D-02's conclusion is unchanged; its evidence base doubles.

Build both halves now with tests. Do not split one gate across two phases.

Output: one detector, one gate method, one dispatch block, one unit test file.
</objective>
