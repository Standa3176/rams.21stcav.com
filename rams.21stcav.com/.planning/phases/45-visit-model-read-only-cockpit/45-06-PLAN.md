application — a logout `<form>` (`resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php:1356`), a command-palette
`<input>`, nav `<button>`s and `@vite`/Alpine `<script>` tags. Those are pre-existing global chrome.
They are **explicitly out of scope** of the read-only fence, by decision recorded here at plan time:
they belong to no part of Phase 45, they are identical on the eleven-tab page, and Plan 45-08
separately asserts by sha256 that this phase did not modify the layout at all. The fence's subject
is the **`cav-brand cav-cockpit` subtree** — every element this phase authors. Nothing in that
subtree is a form control, and nothing in it is a `<script>`.
