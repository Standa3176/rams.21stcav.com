<objective>
Consolidate project-wide narrative storage. Five surgical changes deliver the DATA-01 invariant ("a single project-wide scope edit propagates to ONE canonical JSON location"):

1. **Stop auto-seeding Project.works_description from QuoteWerks PDF overview** — `ExtractRamsDraftJob` (and its companion `RamsExtractionDraftBuilderService`) no longer write `Project.works_description = $extracted['overview']`. The raw QuoteWerks overview still appears in the review form as a starting-value suggestion in the scope textarea, but is not persisted until the PM approves the review form (D-02).
2. **Merge `method_statement_notes` into `scope_of_works`** — `ProjectPackageReviewController::save()` and `approve()` drop the line `'works_description' => $payload['method_statement_notes'] ?? ...` (lines 348 and 393 per audit). The "PM INSTRUCTIONS" separator-suffix block in `MethodStatementService::buildScope()` line 144 is removed — the AI reads `scope_of_works` directly because the PM notes are merged in (D-03).
3. **Drop `reviewed_data.project.overview`** — `RamsReviewDataService::normaliseProject()` line 113 no longer emits the `overview` key. Nothing reads it. (D-09)
4. **Persist `scope_of_works_bullets` at approve-time** — Move the bullet computation from `RamsComplianceUpgradeService::upgradeScopeOfWorks()` (currently run on every PDF render) to `ProjectPackageReviewController::approve()` (run once at approve). The render-time service becomes a read-through cache: when `reviewed_data.scope_of_works_bullets` is non-empty, skip the heuristic. (D-06)
5. **Simplify the per-room fallback chain in `pdf/rams.blade.php`** — line 834 currently reads `$roomOv['overview'] ?? ($roomOv['description'] ?? ($roomOv['scope'] ?? ''))`. Simplify to `$roomOv['overview'] ?? ''` because `description` and `scope` are no longer in the normalised schema after Plan 03 trimmed it. (D-08)
6. **Drop `$room['description']` fallback in `RamsComplianceUpgradeService::ensurePerRoomBullets()` line 71** — pulled forward from Plan 03's Step 5 (which originally tried to touch this file, but `RamsComplianceUpgradeService.php` is owned by this plan). The fallback reads a key (`description`) that Plan 03 has just removed from the canonical schema — drop it here as part of Task 3's same-file touch (D-01).

These six changes are tightly coupled: removing the project-wide narrative auto-seed without also removing the controller-side method_statement_notes mapping would leave the chain half-broken. Therefore they ship as one plan.

Purpose: Closes DATA-01 (single canonical scope location).

Output: The DATA-01 invariant holds — editing scope in one place propagates to one place. RamsRenderRegression still green or skipped (no render drift).
</objective>

| ExtractRamsDraftJob → Project model | Job stops auto-writing `Project.works_description` from raw quote PDF text.
