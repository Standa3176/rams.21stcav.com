# Phase 46.8 Plan 02: Comms Room Out Of The Survey Document And Carry-Forward Summary

Removed comms-room access from the site-survey's own Word document/PDF header and from the survey-to-install carry-forward, per 46.8 D-02's explicit override of 46.5 D-03 -- while leaving `comms_room_access_status`/`comms_room_access_notes` untouched on disk, exactly as 46.8 D-03 requires.

## What Was Built

**Task 1 — `SiteSurveyDocxService` and `_header-meta.blade.php` (commit `9dc0d68e`)**

- `SiteSurveyDocxService::LOGISTICS_KEYS` dropped `'comms_room_access_status'`. The method that builds the Site Logistics table (`buildSiteLogistics()`) reads `SurveyCarryForward::forSurvey()` generically and filters by `LOGISTICS_KEYS` — it has no comms-room-specific branch, so removing the key from the allow-list was the only change needed; the row simply stops being iterated.
- `resources/views/pdf/site-survey/_header-meta.blade.php`: deleted the "Comms room access" `<tr>` block (three checkbox spans + Notes) and the `$commsAccessStatus`/`$commsAccessNotes` variables from the `@php` block above it. The "Distance from base" row and its two variables are untouched.
- `tests/Feature/Documents/SiteSurveyDocxSiteLogisticsTest.php`: `test_comms_room_access_status_renders_the_shared_label_not_the_stored_token` renamed to `test_comms_room_access_status_renders_nothing_and_the_label_map_is_gone` (asserts absence + `COMMS_ROOM_LABELS` no longer `defined()`); `test_site_logistics_labels_and_order_come_from_survey_carry_forward`'s expected key list drops `comms_room_access_status`; `test_site_survey_docx_renders_the_pm_typed_site_logistics` drops the `comms_room_access_notes` assertion from its loop (it would otherwise false-fail); `test_empty_site_logistics_renders_no_heading_and_no_rows` drops `comms_room_access_status` from its key loop (it would otherwise throw on an undefined `FIELDS` key); added `test_comms_room_access_never_renders` as an explicit absence proof.
- `tests/Feature/Documents/SiteSurveyPdfCommsAccessVocabularyTest.php`: rewritten. The four vocabulary tests (`test_yes_ticks_permission_required` etc.) and the two glyph/escaping tests are replaced by `test_comms_room_access_never_renders_for_any_stored_status` (all four stored statuses + null), `test_distance_from_base_row_is_unaffected` (the sibling row proof the plan's non-vacuity section demanded), and `test_the_removal_introduced_no_unescaped_output` (retained — it covers the whole file's raw-echo discipline, not just the removed row).

**Task 2 — `SurveyCarryForward` (commit `44f47296`)**

- Removed `'comms_room_access_status' => 'Comms room access'` from `FIELDS`; deleted `COMMS_ROOM_LABELS`; deleted `commsRoomAccess()`; removed the `'comms_room_access_status' => self::commsRoomAccess($survey)` arm from `forSurvey()`'s `match()`, leaving `distance_from_base_miles`'s own arm and the `default` arm untouched.
- Added a "46.8 D-02 OVERRIDE" section to the class docblock pointing at `46.8-CONTEXT.md` D-02/D-03, so a future reader does not read the surviving "D-01, quoted verbatim" text (which still quotes "comms-room access and notes" as historical record) as still describing current behaviour.
- `tests/Unit/Visits/SurveyCarryForwardTest.php`: `test_fields_const_names_the_ten_d01_columns_in_render_order` renamed to `test_fields_const_names_the_eight_surviving_d01_columns_in_render_order` with the comms-room key dropped from the expected list; added `test_comms_room_access_status_is_never_carried_forward` (asserts `FIELDS` absence, `COMMS_ROOM_LABELS` non-`defined()`, and that a real stored comms-room value never reaches `forProject()`'s output); `test_it_returns_all_ten_fields_labelled_in_render_order` renamed and its comms-room key/label/value assertions replaced with `assertArrayNotHasKey`.
- `tests/Feature/Worksheets/SurveyCarryForwardOnEngineerLinkTest.php`: `test_all_ten_fields_render_on_an_unauthenticated_engineer_link` renamed to `test_eight_surviving_fields_render_on_an_unauthenticated_engineer_link`; the comms-room label/value assertions in its two loops are replaced with `assertDontSee('Comms room access', ...)`, `assertDontSee('SENTINEL-COMMS-ROOM-NOTES', ...)`, and `assertDontSee('Permission required', ...)` — this is the HTTP-level inversion the plan's `<behavior>` required, proved against the real unauthenticated `/worksheet/{token}` page.
