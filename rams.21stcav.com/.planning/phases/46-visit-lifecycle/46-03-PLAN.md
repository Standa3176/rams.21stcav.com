<objective>
**This is the phase's core feature.** A survey's site findings appear on the install engineer's
link, read-only, read live from the survey record and never copied.

Purpose: the milestone exists because this app is good at collecting information from engineers and
bad at turning it into work. Today an install engineer opening `/worksheet/{token}` sees seven site
logistics fields carried from the survey — and does not see what the surveyor recorded about access
constraints, site risks, health and safety, or their own general notes. Those four are the ones that
get somebody hurt or send a van home empty.
Output: one support class that resolves the ten D-01 fields, the existing Site Logistics drawer
widened to render all ten, and a test that proves a change to the survey shows up on the link.
</objective>
