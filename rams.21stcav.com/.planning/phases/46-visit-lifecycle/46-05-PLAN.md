<objective>
Make "send back" mean something on the engineer's side: the survey link reopens for more
information, and both engineer links say the office asked.

Purpose: D-02 says send back "rejects the return and reopens the engineer link for more information,
rather than accepting something incomplete". The obvious implementation — clear the survey's
`submitted_at` — would destroy the record of when the engineer submitted, which is exactly the
"office overwrites engineer-captured data" failure D-02 forbids one line later. This plan does it by
derivation instead.
Output: a reopening that is computed, never stored; eleven gate sites moved from `isSubmitted()` to
`isLockedForEngineer()`; one banner partial, rendered with the office's wording on the engineer-only
survey link and without it on the client-signed worksheet link.
</objective>

    - **Nothing in this plan writes to `site_surveys`, `worksheets`, `worksheet_signoffs` or
      `visits`.** A test asserts all four row counts and the survey's `submitted_at` value are
      unchanged across a reopened render. That assertion IS D-02's "do not let an office note
      overwrite or edit engineer-captured data", made executable.
    - A survey with no visit at all behaves exactly as today: locked once submitted.
  </behavior>
