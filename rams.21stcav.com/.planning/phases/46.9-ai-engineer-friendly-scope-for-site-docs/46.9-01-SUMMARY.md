## Decisions taken where the plan offered a choice

**Test E — normalise, not discard.** A non-dashed AI line is prefixed with `- ` and persisted. The
reason is written into `dashPrefix()`'s docblock: `WorksheetGeneratorService:111,174` gate the
**block**, so a block with no dashed line is discarded wholesale, while the survey
(`SurveyController:485-496`) just splits on newlines — a non-dashed summary would render on the
survey and vanish from the worksheet. Discarding would also throw the usable AI content away and
leave the room with nothing. Normalising is visually free because
`WorksheetGeneratorService:578` strips the leading dash again when it builds
`works_summary_bullets`.

**`$timeout = 240`, minted new rather than reusing 300.** The plan offered either. I took 240 and
cited it honestly in the docblock: it is justified by the 2×180s = 360s worst case arithmetic
alone, and the docblock records explicitly that **no job in `app/Jobs/` uses 240** (the existing
set is 60, 120, 120, 180, 180, 300×4, 600) and that `BuildWorksheetJob:36`'s 300 was the available
precedent. The plan's earlier claim that `ReimportQuoteJob:67` is a 240 precedent is wrong — that
line is 180 — and the plan already says so.

## Where source contradicted the plan / CONTEXT

1. **C-04's wording is imprecise, and the imprecision matters.** CONTEXT.md and the plan both say
   `WorksheetGeneratorService:111,174` *"discard any `works_summary` line not dash-prefixed"*. They
   do not. Those two lines gate the whole **block**
   (`str_starts_with($bull, '- ') || str_contains($bull, "\n- ")`), and once a block passes,
   `:573-579` keeps **every** non-empty line and merely strips a leading `-`/`•`. So the real
   failure mode is coarser and worse than described: a block with **no** dashed line anywhere is
   dropped **in full**, not line by line. The implemented rule (normalise every line) satisfies
   both the block gate and the plan's own must-have truth *"Every bullet line the job persists
   starts with '- '"*, so the correction does not change the behaviour built — but the citation
   should be fixed before 46.9-03 re-states it.
2. **CONTEXT D-03 cites `:51-57` for the empty-overview wipe; the real lines are `:47-55`.** The
   plan already corrects this, and `:47-55` is what the test and the job's comment cite.
3. **CONTEXT D-06(b) cites `ReimportQuoteJob:67` as a 240 precedent.** It is 180. The plan already
   flags this; recorded again so the CONTEXT line is not inherited.

None of these required a deviation from the plan's instructions.
