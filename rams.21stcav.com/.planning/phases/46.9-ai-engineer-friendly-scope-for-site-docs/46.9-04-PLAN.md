**1 — The cost figure behind your own decision was wrong. You should know the real number.**

On 2026-10-10 you chose "run it in the background right after import" from three options, and part of
what you were told was *"each room is its own AI call, so a 12-room quote is 12 calls."* **That is
wrong.** `RoomOverviewSummaryService:59` makes **one** `AIManager::run()` call with every room in a
single payload, outside any loop. A 12-room quote is **1 AI call, not 12.** Independently verified
twice.

The background-at-import choice still stands on **latency** — the import request does not wait on an
AI round trip, and that reason is unaffected. But you decided partly on a number that was not right,
so: **do you still want it in the background at import, now that it is one cheap call?** The
alternative you were offered was inline at import, which would make the import wait a few seconds and
would remove the job entirely.

One real consequence of the batch shape, in case it matters to you: the single call has a 2000-token
output ceiling (`app/Core/AI/Prompts/RoomOverviewSummaryPrompt.php:95`), and on a very large quote — roughly 12+ rooms — the
response can be truncated, at which point the service falls back for **all** rooms at once rather
than some. We have not measured your real room counts; that is item 6 below.

**2 — A room with no memo: nothing, or thin bullets? (D-11 — your call to overturn.)**

Some QuoteWerks rooms have an empty `CustomMemo01`. For those rooms, **every** per-room source is
empty. The only non-empty text available is the quote-wide scope narrative, which is **identical for
every room on the quote**.

Built behaviour: **generate nothing.** The engineer still gets bullets — machine-split from whatever
scope prose is available, labelled honestly on the page as *"Install actions (from scope notes)"*.

The reason for refusing: feeding a quote-wide narrative into a prompt that is told to name the room
and not to invent kit is an invitation to put **the wrong kit in the wrong room**, on a document an
engineer works from and a client may see. That is the worst available outcome here.

**Do you want bullets everywhere, even thin or repeated ones, or a blank where we genuinely do not
know?** You may reasonably prefer the former; it is your document.

**3 — Should re-imports also generate? (About three lines.)**

Today the job fires on the **QuoteWerks import** path only. The PDF-async import path already had
this feature (`ExtractQuoteJob::generateContentPack()`). But `reimport()` and
`completePendingReimport()` — re-running extraction on an existing quote — generate **nothing**, and
still would not after this phase.

So: re-import a quote and the bullets do not refresh. **Should those two paths dispatch too?** Cost
is about three lines. The argument against is only that a re-import on a package where the office has
since written summaries would generate nothing anyway (D-03 protects them), so the benefit is limited
to genuinely blank rooms.

**4 — Something you have not been shown: the engineer link cannot tell AI bullets from your words.**

`SurveyController:491` labels **any** non-empty `works_summary` as `planned_actions_source = 'office'`.
So once the job writes bullets, the engineer link presents them exactly as it presents office-authored
ones. We have asserted this as the real behaviour rather than glossed it, but we have not changed it —
the fence says no Blade edit.

Related, and deferred (item 5a): the office review screen has **no positive "AI-written" marker**
either. Half the mechanism exists — a `_summary_fallback` flag already drives an *"AI unavailable —
click Generate to retry"* badge at `project-packages/review.blade.php:875-879` — but there is nothing
that says *"a machine wrote this, check it before you send it."* **Do you want one?** Not in this
phase; naming it so it is a decision rather than an omission.

**5 — Two things CONTEXT deliberately deferred. Confirm they stay deferred.**

- **5a — Labelling AI-written summaries to the office.** As above.
- **5b — The project-level scope as a second surface.** Your request said *"project scope of works /
  space descriptions"*. This phase did the **per-room** path. There is a second, genuinely separate
  path — `SurveyService::resolveProjectScopeForSurvey()` feeding the survey's general notes — which
  **none** of these decisions covers and which this phase did not touch. **Confirm it is a separate
  ask, or say now if it was what you actually meant.**

**6 — One measurement nobody has: your real room counts and memo fill rate.**

How many rooms a typical quote has, and how often `CustomMemo01` is actually filled, decides whether
items 1 and 2 are edge cases or the normal case. **Nobody has measured it.** It needs a query against
the live QuoteWerks replica or a count over existing `project_packages`. Say the word and it becomes a
quick task; it is not in this phase.

**7 — What to actually look at, if you want to see it work.** Import a QuoteWerks quote with at least
one memo-filled room and one memo-less room, give the queue worker a moment, then open the survey
engineer link for that project on a phone. The memo-filled room should read as short imperative
bullets; the memo-less one should read as *"Install actions (from scope notes)"*. If a room already
had an office-written summary, it must be **unchanged, word for word**.
