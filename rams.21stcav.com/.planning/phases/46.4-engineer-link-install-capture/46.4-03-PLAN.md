<objective>
Build the two screens the office reads: the **asset list** (D-05) and the **additional-kit list**
(the acceptance test, in the user's words — *"presented to the office admin as a clean list rather
than free text"*).

onto `Device`. What has never existed is a place to READ it. And additional kit has never existed at
all; today it lands in a `max:5000` comments textarea or a phone call. **This plan is where the
phase is judged.**

Output: a per-project asset list with a CSV export, and a per-worksheet additional-kit table with a
reconcile tick.

**This plan runs in parallel with 46.4-02.** It shares not one file with it: 02 owns
`public-show.blade.php` and `PublicWorksheetController.php`; this plan owns the office side.
</objective>
