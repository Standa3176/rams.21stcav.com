<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <what-built>
    The engineer link is now an install-capture tool. Each room has a **Start** tray and a
    **Completion** tray, each photo can carry a **label**, and each room has an **Add additional
    kit** drawer where the engineer picks their name from those allocated to the visit, types a
    quantity and a part description, and taps Add — as many times as needed. Kit rows **queue
    offline** and upload themselves when signal returns. The office gets two new screens: the
    project **asset list** (built on the serial capture that already existed), and the per-worksheet
    **additional-kit table**, which can be ticked as reconciled.

    Deliberately NOT built, and each for a stated reason: no photo on a kit row (your words —
    *"dont need kit pics, on serial cpature"*); no second URL for the client (D-01); no room rename;
    no hard photo gate — every gate on this page stays visual, because engineers on flaky networks
    must not be locked out.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    **1. The acceptance test — the office list. Do this one first; it is what the phase is for.**
    Open a worksheet that has a few additional-kit rows on it and put the quote next to it.
    - Can you reconcile the kit against the quote **reading only the screen, re-keying nothing**?
    - Is the engineer's name right, and is it obvious when no engineer was allocated?
    - Is anything missing that you would have phoned about?

    **2. The engineer link on a phone, in a room, one-handed.**
    Open `/worksheet/{token}` on a phone. In one room: add a start photo with a label, add a
    completion photo, then open Add additional kit and add **three** items without closing the
    drawer. Then delete one.
    - Did the label field get in the way, or earn its place?
    - Are the two trays obviously different things, or did you have to read them?
    - Is the engineer picker right — is your name in it?

    **3. The bad-connection walk. THIS IS THE ONE THAT CANNOT BE PROVEN BY A TEST.**
    Put the phone in **airplane mode**. Add **one photo** and **one kit row**. Both should say
    `📥 Saved offline`. Check the pending chip shows **2** and the panel names them correctly (a
    🧰 for the kit row, with its qty and description — not "Completed-work photo").
    Now turn signal back on and wait up to a minute.
    - **Did BOTH arrive?** A kit row stuck at "Local blob unreadable" is the failure this whole
      phase was designed around — if you see it, stop and say so.
    - Reload the page: are both really there, or did only the toast say so?

    **4. Photos.** Tap a thumbnail. It should open. (It has done nothing at all until now — that was
    a pre-existing bug taken into scope this phase.)

    **5. Buckets and history.** Open a worksheet that had photos **before** this deploy. They should
    all sit in **Completion**, because the tray they were captured through was literally titled
    "Photos of completed work". Is that the right call, or would you rather they were marked as
    unbucketed?

    **6. The client's view.** This is the SAME page and the SAME URL the client signs. Read it as a
    client would. Is the additional-kit list something you are happy for them to see? D-01 says yes
    — this is your last cheap chance to say no, before anything is built on top of it.
  </how-to-verify>
