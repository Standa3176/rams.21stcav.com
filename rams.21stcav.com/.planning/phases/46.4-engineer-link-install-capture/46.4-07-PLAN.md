<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <what-built>
    The engineer link is now an install-capture tool. Each room has a **Start** tray and a
    **Completion** tray, each photo can carry a **label**, and each room has an **Add additional
    kit** drawer where the engineer picks their name from those allocated to the visit, types a
    quantity and a part description, and taps Add — as many times as needed. They can also
    **correct** a row, and **mark one for deletion with a reason** — the row stays on the list,
    flagged, so the office is told rather than finding a hole. Adds **queue offline** and upload
    themselves when signal returns.

    **Once you sign, the page goes read-only.** No more photos, labels, deletes or kit changes —
    enforced on the server, not just by hiding buttons. Signing again still works.

    The office gets two new screens: the project **asset list** (built on the serial capture that
    already existed), and the per-worksheet **additional-kit table**, where an added, amended and
    marked row each read as what they are, and each can be ticked as reconciled.

    Deliberately NOT built, each for a stated reason: no photo on a kit row (*"dont need kit pics,
    on serial cpature"*); no unit field (you declined it); no second URL for the client (D-01, and
    D-07's lock is why it is not needed); no room rename; no hard delete anywhere; no hard photo
    gate — every gate on this page stays visual, because engineers on flaky networks must not be
    locked out.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    **1. The acceptance test — the office list. Do this one first; it is what the phase is for.**
    Open a worksheet with a few kit rows — including one **amended** and one **marked** — and put
    the quote next to it.
    - Can you reconcile the kit against the quote **reading only the screen, re-keying nothing**?
    - Does a marked row tell you enough to act on it, or would you still have to phone?
    - Does an amended row show you what changed, or just the latest numbers?
    - The **asset list** is the least specified thing in this phase and had no decision behind its
      shape. Is it what you wanted, or is it "not that"?

    **2. The engineer link on a phone, in a room, one-handed.**
    Add a start photo with a label, a completion photo, then open Add additional kit and add
    **three** items without closing the drawer. Then **correct** one, and **mark** another with a
    reason.
    - Is the engineer picker right — is your name in it?
    - Did having to type a reason feel like friction or like the right amount of ceremony?
    - Are the two photo trays obviously different things, or did you have to read them?

    **3. The bad-connection walk. THIS IS THE ONE THAT CANNOT BE PROVEN BY A TEST.**
    Airplane mode. Add **one photo** and **one kit row**. Both should say `📥 Saved offline`. The
    pending chip should show **2** and the panel should name them correctly (a 🧰 for the kit row,
    with its qty and description — not "Completed-work photo").
    Now try to **correct** a row that is already on the server: it should be visibly disabled and
    say **`Needs a connection`** — not fail silently. A row still in the queue should offer
    **Edit** and **Discard**, and those should work.
    Then restore signal and wait up to a minute.
    - **Did BOTH arrive?** A kit row stuck at "Local blob unreadable" is the failure this whole
      phase was designed around — if you see it, stop and say so.
    - Reload: are both really there, or did only the toast say so?
    - ⚠ **Is "you need signal to correct or retract a row" acceptable?** That is a real limitation
      and it was my call. If it is not, the fix is a queued-modify protocol as its own phase.

    **4. The sign-off lock (D-07).** Sign the worksheet as the client would. Then try to add a
    photo, add kit, correct a row and mark a row.
    - Are the controls gone, and does the page say **why** in a sentence you would be happy for a
      client to read?
    - Can you still read everything you signed?
    - Sign again — does it still work?

    **5. Buckets and history.** Open a worksheet that had photos **before** this deploy. They should
    all sit in **Completion**, because the tray they were captured through was literally titled
    "Photos of completed work". Right call, or would you rather they were marked unbucketed?

    **6. Two rulings I made that you have not been asked about.**
    - **Marking room-complete and survey-reviewed still work after sign-off.** They are engineer
      status confirmations, not the captured record, and D-07 does not name them. Should they lock
      too?
    - **There is no way to UNMARK a row.** If an engineer marks one by mistake, the office sorts it
      out. You did not ask for an unmark and I did not invent one. Do you want one?

    **7. Tapping a photo now opens it.** It did nothing at all before — a pre-existing bug taken
    into scope.
