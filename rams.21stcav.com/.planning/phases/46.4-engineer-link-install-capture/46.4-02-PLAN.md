<objective>
Give every room a **Start** tray and a **Completion** tray (D-03), let the engineer **label** a photo
as they capture it (D-04), and — because this phase makes photos the centre of the page — make
tapping a thumbnail actually open it.

Purpose: D-04 is nearly free (`caption` is already fillable and already validated server-side; the
front end simply never sends it) and D-03 is one new column that plan 01 already shipped. Doing both
in one pass over the photo tray means the tray is rewritten once, not twice.

Output: two trays per room, a label field, a vanilla lightbox, and a server that validates the two
new inputs.

**REVISED 2026-09-26 after D-07.** The capture surface must become **read-only once a
`WorksheetSignoff` exists** — photo upload, photo label and photo delete included. That lock is
**plan 46.4-04's** job, applied in one place across every capture endpoint, and it lands one wave
after this plan. **Do not build a second, partial lock here.** What this plan owes 04 is only that
the two new trays and the label field are marked up so a single guard can hide them: give each
capture control a shared hook (`data-capture-control`) and keep every new write on the existing
`/photos` endpoint rather than inventing another. Note in the SUMMARY that the trays ship unlocked
for exactly one wave.
</objective>
