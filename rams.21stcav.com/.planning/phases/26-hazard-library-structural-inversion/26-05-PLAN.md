<objective>
Close the HAZ-04 gap RESEARCH identified as the real work: `quote-review.blade.php`'s hazard row is currently a Low/Medium/High select mapped to coarse `[preL,preS]` pairs, with no numeric input and no way to tell "the app filled this in and nobody looked" from "an engineer confirmed this." Extend the schema end-to-end (extraction shape from Plan 26-04 -> `RamsReviewDataService` normaliser -> review form -> `reviewedToRisk()` -> `generated_data`) so typical scores are visibly pre-filled, editable, and travel with a reviewed/unreviewed marker; and give D-06's needs_confirmation flag a visible badge.

Purpose: HAZ-04 is explicit — "Do not let the app apply the typical scores silently." Without this plan, Plan 26-04's numeric scores would reach the review screen but the screen would still only expose a 3-bucket select, silently discarding precision and never distinguishing reviewed from unreviewed.
Output: RamsReviewDataService::normaliseHazards() with the extended schema; RamsBuilderService::reviewedToRisk() carrying score_reviewed through; quote-review.blade.php with numeric inputs, the reviewed marker, and the confirmation badge.
</objective>
