<objective>
D-09: tab persistence and autosave for the now-flattened survey link follow the **worksheet's**
(Phase 46.7) mechanics — `location.hash` + `sessionStorage` for the active tab, `localStorage`-only
for the offline draft — rather than SCC's own (weaker: no reload persistence at all) or inventing a
third approach. This is the behavioural centre of D-09, sequenced after 46.6-03's structural flatten
so the diff here is "how saving/navigating works," not "what the page looks like."

⚠️ Today's survey link already has PARTIAL debounced autosave (Alpine `$watch` on specific
`_ui`/`engineer_feedback` fields, firing `stepSave` on a 600ms debounce — `show.blade.php:1917`
onward) but it is gated to `screen === 'step'` (a property this phase is retiring) and has **no
offline fallback at all** — a failed `fetch` just sets `saveError` text and does nothing else. This
plan's job is narrower than "build autosave from scratch": re-point the existing debounce at the
flattened sections' fields (no longer gated by `screen`), and ADD the missing localStorage-backed
offline queue + "Needs a connection" messaging the worksheet already proved out.

Purpose: the house rule stated plainly by both this phase's CONTEXT and 46.7's own D-04/D-06 — no
affordance may appear to work offline and silently lose the change.

Output: the retired `screen`-gated autosave trigger replaced by a flatten-aware one; a new
`localStorage` draft queue; `location.hash`/`sessionStorage` tab persistence added to 46.6-01's
`activeTab` switch.
</objective>
