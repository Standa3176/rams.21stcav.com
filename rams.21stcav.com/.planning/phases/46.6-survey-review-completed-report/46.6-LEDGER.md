## Task 1b — the three previously-unreachable reports, now reachable

Measured before this plan touched anything: `grep -rn "completed-report" resources/views/` returned
**zero hits**. After: `resources/views/site-survey/edit.blade.php` carries all three report links
(`site-surveys.completed-report`, `site-surveys.completed-report.xlsx`, and the pre-existing,
previously-orphaned `site-surveys.client-report`), placed **outside** `<form id="survey-form">`
(HTML forbids nested forms — 46.6-02's own Completed Report panel hit this first and placed its
forms the same way), as plain `<a href="...">` GET downloads, never a `<form>`.

Labelled unambiguously per D-05: the completed report carries `— Internal` beside both its PDF and
`.xlsx` links; the pre-existing client report carries `— Client-facing`. Getting those two the
wrong way round would send an internal-only document to a client.

**Non-vacuity**: `SurveyReportLinksReachableTest::test_the_rendered_edit_page_carries_all_three_report_links`
renders the REAL edit page for a completed survey and asserts each of the three generated URLs is
a substring of the response body — not `Route::has(...)`, which was already `true` and is exactly
what left them unreachable. `::test_following_the_completed_report_xlsx_link_returns_a_non_error_response`
then actually follows one of them and asserts a genuine `200`/spreadsheet content-type. Proven RED
first: before the Blade edit, 2 of 4 tests failed with `To contain: …/completed-report` — the real
failure mode, not a fixture problem.
