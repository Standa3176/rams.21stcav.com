<objective>
Move the four review controls out of Overview and put them beneath the evidence, where D-02 says
they belong. The user's reason is the design rule: **a PM should not be able to accept a visit
without having looked at it.**

Purpose: criterion 3. Today Accept sits on a row that shows a date, a title and a status chip, and
nothing that came back from site. After this plan, pressing Accept means scrolling past the photos,
the room answers, the serials and the client's signature to reach it.

Output: one new prop on `visit-row`, two call sites, and the repointing of every existing
assertion that judged those controls on Overview — done deliberately, in one commit, with the move
recorded at each site rather than tests quietly edited until green.
</objective>

THE FINDING THAT MAKES THIS MOVE SAFE — verified in `database/factories/VisitFactory.php` on
2026-09-21, and it must be re-proved by test rather than trusted:
`returned()` creates a `Worksheet` source when the visit has none, and BOTH `sentBack()` and
`accepted()` are built on `returned()`. `Visit::returnedAt()` reads the SOURCE and returns null when
there is none. Therefore **no visit can reach RETURNED, SENT_BACK or ACCEPTED without a
`source_type`** — and a visit with a `source_type` is exactly the set for which Plan 46.1-03 offers
the Returned tab. So relocating the controls onto that tab can lose nothing. Prove it; do not
assume it.
