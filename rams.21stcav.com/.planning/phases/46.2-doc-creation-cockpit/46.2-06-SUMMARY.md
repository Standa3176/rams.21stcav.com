# Phase 46.2 Plan 06: The end-to-end walk and the phase close Summary

**The phase walks end to end through HTTP, two of the four documents are proven to open for real, and
the other two are recorded NOT VERIFIED by name with the reason.** The fence was re-proved capable of
failing on this commit with three separate reds, the visit work was driven at its own routes beside a
page that links nowhere near it, and every count the phase moved is in one ledger — which also
carries **a retraction of this plan's own false finding**, because that is the more useful record.

## ⛔ The spend boundary, stated first because it changes what this plan proved

The plan's headline verification is *"generate each of the four documents on a real local project and
confirm the artifact opens."* **Two were done. Two were not.**

`BuildRamsDocumentJob`, `BuildOmManualJob` and `BuildWorksheetJob` **step 1**
(`WorksheetGeneratorService::generateContent()` — per-room AI) all call the model. Running them costs
real money on the user's Anthropic account and **that spend was not authorised.**

| Document | Word artifact | PDF artifact |
|---|---|---|
| **Worksheet** | ✅ **PRODUCED AND OPENED** | n/a — DC-07, no renderer exists |
| **Site survey** | ✅ **PRODUCED AND OPENED** | ❌ **NOT VERIFIED** — Browsershot, not authorised |
| **RAMS** | ❌ **NOT VERIFIED** | ❌ **NOT VERIFIED** |
| **O&M manual** | ❌ **NOT VERIFIED** | ❌ **NOT VERIFIED** |

**Four of the eight format cells have never been opened by anyone. Reason: unauthorised AI spend
(RAMS, O&M) and unauthorised Browsershot render (site-survey PDF).** Nobody reading a green phase
should conclude otherwise.

For the RAMS and the O&M, everything short of the bytes IS proven: the POST validates, the values
land in the columns the generator reads, the delegation is reached (`Bus::assertDispatched`), and
every format the PM can choose resolves to a route that exists.

### Two things that make the boundary an assertion instead of a promise

1. **`QUEUE_CONNECTION` is `sync` in `phpunit.xml`.** An un-faked `dispatch()` RUNS the job inline. So
   the plan's literal instruction — *"`Queue::fake()` is NOT enough here … run the job inline"* — is
   precisely the instruction that would have spent the money, and it was **deviated from in the
   opposite direction**: `Bus::fake()` first in every test that reaches a generator, with
   `Bus::assertDispatched()` standing in for the run. The dispatch IS the delegation, and the
   delegation is what this phase owns.
2. **`Http::fake()` in `setUp()`** is the second belt — nothing may leave the machine even if a future
   generator stops going through the bus. It also means that **if either proven Word path ever
   acquires a model call, the test goes red rather than paying for it.**

### The site-survey premise was wrong, was corrected, and cost nothing to close

The brief that carried the prohibition named the site survey alongside the RAMS and the O&M as
"running through the AI pipeline". **It does not.** `site-surveys.docx` →
`SiteSurveyController::downloadDocx` → `SiteSurveyDocxService::build()` is pure PhpWord plus
`DocumentTemplateService`, with no model call anywhere in it — the same class of thing as
`WorksheetDocxService`.

The first pass of this plan **reported that and declined to act on it**, because the instruction
named the site survey explicitly and a prohibition on the user's money is not an executor's to
reinterpret on a premise. The coordinator checked the premise, corrected it, and authorised the Word
cell only. It is now proven, and **DC-06 went from one opened artifact to two at zero cost.**

## 🔴 RETRACTED: `D-46.2-06-04` was a FALSE FINDING

**This plan reported that `DEFERRED_AFFORDANCES` was the one fence list whose length is not asserted.
That was wrong. It is asserted, it always has been, and it bites.**

It is the **first** assertion in `test_the_fence_enumerates_the_whole_deferred_set()` — the test whose
name says exactly that — and the other three lists are pinned 110 lines further down **the same
method**, at `:964-966`.

### How the false finding was made, because the method is the lesson

The reconciliation was done with a single-line regex:

```
$ grep -n "assertCount([0-9]\|assertSame([0-9]" tests/Feature/Cockpit/CockpitReadOnlyFenceTest.php
964:        $this->assertCount(2, self::FORBIDDEN_MARKUP);
965:        $this->assertCount(13, self::WRITE_SURFACE_TABLES);
```

Three hits, so three pinned lists, so the fourth must be unpinned. **The three that matched are
single-line calls; the one that did not is a multi-line call with its `21` on its own line**, which
`assertCount([0-9]` cannot match. **An absence in a grep was read as an absence in the code**, and a
paragraph was then written asserting a negative that had never been checked by any other means.

The claim propagated into the count ledger, the retirement ledger, REQUIREMENTS DC-11, this summary,
and a report that led the coordinator to authorise a commit adding the "missing" assertion.

## D-46.2-04-02 — assessed as IN SCOPE, and fixed

`resources/views/pdf/site-survey/_header-meta.blade.php` compared `comms_room_access_status` against
`permission|outsourced|free`, while the stored vocabulary is `yes,no,outsourced,unknown` — validated
in three controllers (`PublicSurveyController:759`, `SiteSurveyController:681`,
`SurveyController:1096`) and mapped correctly by every other renderer (`WorksheetDocxService:156`,
`DocxBuilderService:711`, both `yes => 'Permission required'`, `no => 'Free access'`). So
**"Permission required" and "Free" could never tick**, whatever the surveyor answered.

**Why in scope:** 46.2-05 put the field on the cockpit's own document form, turning a defect nobody
could trigger into one a PM triggers in a click. Logging it a third time would have been a decision to
ship a known-wrong document off a brand-new surface.

**Measured RED first:** `Tests: 5 failed, 1 passed`. After: `Tests: 11 passed` across
`tests/Feature/Documents` (was 5).

### The second defect in the same three lines

The glyphs were `{{ … '&#9745;' : '&#9744;' }}`, and `{{ }}` runs `e()` with `$doubleEncode = true`, so
the entity left the renderer as `&amp;#9744;` and **the PDF printed the literal text `&#9744;`**.
Measured by rendering the Blade, not reasoned about:

```
<span class="checkbox">&amp;#9744;</span> Permission required &nbsp;
<span class="checkbox">&amp;#9744;</span> Outsourced &nbsp;
<span class="checkbox">&amp;#9744;</span> Free &nbsp;
```

Fixing only the comparison would have produced a tick **no reader could see**, so the two are one fix.
The glyphs are now the ballot **characters** (U+2611 / U+2610) — no entity, survives `e()`, and **no
`{!! !!}` added** to a PDF Blade, which is the exact thing T-46.2-16 exists to keep out.

**What changed and nothing else:** three comparison lines, their glyph pairs, and the stale
`// permission|outsourced|free|null` comment. No generator. `unknown` and `null` tick nothing, which
is correct — there is no fourth box, and adding one is a content decision.
