  <name>Task 1: The walk — one visit, in order, on one worksheet</name>
  <files>tests/Feature/Worksheets/EngineerLinkTabbedWalkthroughTest.php</files>
  <behavior>
    A three-room fixture, one worksheet, the steps in the order an engineer does them. Every step goes
    through the **route table** with a real HTTP call, never a direct controller call — a route
    constraint or a throttle key that is wrong is a real defect and a controller call hides it.
    After each step, re-render the page and assert the state, counting what was measured.

    1. **Open the link.** Exactly one active panel; it is the first incomplete room (not the site tab,
       because rooms are outstanding); the bar has 1 site + 3 room buttons; each button's
       `data-room-status` matches that room's real state.
    2. **Read the site tab.** Client, reference, address and the site-contact block all present; the
       `N of M rooms marked complete` chip reads `0 of 3`.
    3. **Type a note in room 2.** POST notes → 200. Re-render: room 2's textarea contains the note,
       rooms 1 and 3's are **empty** (a note leaking across rooms is the kind of defect a
       single-state assertion misses), and `survey_review` / `room_complete` are untouched.
    4. **Mark room 2's survey reviewed.** Re-render: room 2's bar button flips out of `unreviewed`;
       rooms 1 and 3 do **not** move. The note typed in step 3 is **still there** — a full-page POST
       must not lose it.
    5. **Upload a photo to room 2.** Re-render: room 2's status is `in-progress`, its photo count
       incremented by exactly 1, every other room's count unchanged, and the note still present.
    6. **Mark room 2 complete.** Re-render: room 2's button is `complete`, the site chip reads
       `1 of 3`, and room 2's panel carries the skip-restore attribute so a reload will not
       auto-activate it.
    7. **Add a kit row to room 2** (untouched by this phase — assert it still works inside a tab, which
       is the regression this step exists to catch): row visible in room 2's panel and nowhere else.
    8. **Sign off.** Re-render: **zero** `[data-room-notes]` textareas anywhere; the step-3 note still
       **visible as escaped text**; zero tray `[data-capture-control]`; the bar still renders with the
       same 4 buttons and the same statuses; and `ip_address` / `user_agent` / `captured_by` appear
       **nowhere** in the response body.
    9. **Post a note after sign-off.** 422, the lock's sentence verbatim, and
       `pre_install_confirmations` byte-identical to before the attempt.

    Plus two ordering/composition facts that only a sequence can prove:
    - The note from step 3 survives steps 4, 5, 6 and 8 — assert it at each, not only at the end. A
      note lost by the third redirect is a defect no single-state test can see.
    - A **forged** room name at every room-scoped step (notes, reviewed, complete) → 422, nothing
      written. ⚠️ Except `uploadPhoto`, which has **no** room-name guard — a known pre-existing gap.
      Assert the gap's **current** behaviour so this file is an honest record of it, with a comment
      saying it is deferred and deliberately not fixed here.

    Non-vacuity gate, first test in the file: the opening render exceeds 50,000 characters and contains
    the project name.
  </behavior>

<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 3: BLOCKING CHECKPOINT — judged on a phone, one-handed, on a bad connection</name>
  <what-built>
Rooms are now **tabs** in a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen, and **each tab shows whether that
room is done** — that bar is the point of the phase, not the tabs. A **site tab** carries the client,
the reference, the address and the site contact. Each room has a **notes field that saves itself** —
no button to tap. With no signal the words are **held on the phone**, the field says so, and it still
says so after a reload; they send themselves when signal returns. If the client has already signed,
the server refuses the note and you read why, in a sentence, **and the words stay on screen**. The tab
you are on **survives** the reload that a photo upload and a sign-off still do.

Nothing about photo stages, tray labels, kit rows, serial capture or sign-off was changed. No pending
photo already queued on any phone was put at risk — autosave uses a different, separate store on the
device, on purpose.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
⚠️ **JUDGE THIS ON A PHONE, HELD IN ONE HAND, ON A BAD CONNECTION. That is the only place this page is
used.** A laptop browser at a desk cannot fail any of the things that matter here.

Open a real worksheet engineer link on your phone.

1. **The bar, one-handed.** Can your thumb reach every room tab without moving your grip? Scroll it
   sideways if there are more rooms than fit. Does a tap ever land on the wrong room? Does the bar sit
   over the Mark Room Complete or Sign Off button anywhere — try the very bottom of the longest room.
2. **⚠️ THE OFFLINE WALK — the one that matters most.** Turn on **airplane mode**. Open a room, type
   two sentences of notes. What does the field tell you? **Pull down to reload the page.** Are your
   words still there, and does it still say they have not been sent? Now turn airplane mode **off**
   and wait. Do the words send themselves, and does the label change to say so?
3. **Signal-restore with a photo queued too.** Airplane mode on. Take a photo in a room **and** type a
   note **and** add a kit row. Airplane mode off. **Do all three arrive?** (The photo and the kit row
   use the old queue; the note uses the new one. They must not disturb each other.)
4. **The tab survives a photo.** Go to the **last** room, scroll half way down, take a photo. When the
   page comes back — are you still in that room, still roughly where you were? Or did it dump you in
   room 1?
5. **The blocked-sign-off shortcut.** On a worksheet with an unreviewed survey room, tap
   **"Jump to first unreviewed room →"** in the amber banner. Does it take you to that room's tab?
6. **Status at a glance.** Without opening anything, can you tell from the bar which rooms are done and
   which are not? Is it obvious without relying on colour alone? Is the "N of M rooms complete" count
   on the site tab right?
7. **After sign-off.** On a signed worksheet: can you still read every room's notes, photos, labels and
   kit? Can you still move between tabs? And is there genuinely **nothing** left that looks like it
   would let you change anything?
8. **The question we need your answer on.** You asked for autosave. Before this phase there was
   almost **nothing on the page an engineer typed** that autosave could apply to — the photo label
   rides with the photo, the kit rows have their own save, and the comments box is the **client's**.
   So we **added a per-room notes field** and that is what autosave saves. **Is that what you wanted?**
   If you wanted the tabs and the bar and no new field, say so — the field and its endpoint come out and
   nothing else changes.
9. **And confirm the offline ruling still reads right to you now you have used it.** You chose:
   *keep typing offline, words held on the phone, clearly labelled.* The cost, restated: an unsent note
   lives only on **that phone in that browser** — a wiped phone, cleared browsing data, or a different
   handset loses it; and if the client signs before it gets out, the office will not receive it (you
   will be shown why, and the text stays on screen to read out). **Still the right trade?**
  </how-to-verify>
